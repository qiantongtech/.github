**Qiantong Technology** specializes in the **research and development of enterprise-grade data and artificial intelligence infrastructure software**, with a mission to make enterprise data truly intelligent. Centered on the **qData Data Platform Product Suite** and complemented by foundational software products such as qOntology, qKnow, and qModel, the company provides an integrated platform ecosystem connecting sensing, data, knowledge, models, and intelligent applications. This ecosystem supports enterprise data governance, data asset management and value realization, and the practical deployment of AI applications.

The company is committed to the open-source principles of **openness and sharing, technological innovation, ecosystem collaboration, and long-term evolution**. By continuously developing and maintaining open-source projects such as qData and qKnow, we work with developers and ecosystem partners to build an open, reliable, and sustainable enterprise-grade data and AI open-source ecosystem.

### Qiantong Intelligent Platform Product Portfolio

- **qData — Enterprise Data Platform Suite:** Provides integrated capabilities for data integration, development, governance, modeling, asset management, and data services, forming an enterprise-grade data infrastructure foundation.
- **qOntology — Ontology Engineering Platform:** Enables semantic modeling and ontology governance for business objects, attributes, relationships, and rules, establishing an enterprise-grade semantic infrastructure.
- **qKnow — Knowledge and AI Agent Development Platform:** Integrates knowledge graphs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, and workflow orchestration to support enterprise knowledge management and intelligent application development.
- **qModel — Algorithm and Model Management Platform:** Provides capabilities for algorithm and model registration, management, evaluation, deployment, and service delivery, enabling the accumulation and reuse of model assets.
- **qLabel — Data Annotation Platform:** Supports multi-format data annotation, task collaboration, quality review, and dataset management.
- **qThing — Internet of Things (IoT) Platform:** Supports device connectivity, protocol adaptation, data acquisition, and device management, providing a reliable foundation for IoT sensing and data collection.
- **qAuth — Unified Identity and Access Management Platform:** Provides centralized authentication, Single Sign-On (SSO), user and organizational management, access control, and security auditing.
- **qBrain — Industry-Specific Large Language Model Development Platform:** Focuses on capabilities for industry-specific model training, adaptation, evaluation, deployment, and model serving.
